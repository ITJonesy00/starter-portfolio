"Smie" means "forge" or "blacksmith's workshop" in Norwegian. Here, it is about shaping tools, methods, and projects: slowly when necessary, leaving traces, conducting tests, and making deliberate choices.

Each module is its own repository with a clear scope, written in English and built to be reproduced.

"Meridian" is Smie's methodological framework, serving as a guiding compass.
